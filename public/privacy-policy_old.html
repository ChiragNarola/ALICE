App Privacy Policy

Early Years AI Ltd  ·  Applies to: ALICE AI Mobile Application

At A.L.I.C.E., your privacy matters to us. Whether you're a parent or a practitioner we’re committed to protecting your data and being transparent about how we use it.
1

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Early Years AI Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your data when you use the ALICE AI mobile app. This policy applies only to the mobile app, not to our website or other services.

2

Information We Collect Through the App

When you use ALICE AI, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, Email address, Login credentials
  • Child Profile Information (entered by you): Age, Interests, Developmental concerns. You should not enter sensitive personal data such as full medical records, addresses, or identifiable third-party information.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Questions and prompts you enter, Responses generated, App usage patterns (e.g. features used, session activity)
  • Technical Data: Device type, Operating system, App performance data (e.g. crashes, errors)
3

How We Use Your Data

We use data collected through the app to:

  • Provide AI-generated responses
  • Personalise your experience
  • Improve the quality of responses
  • Maintain and improve app performance
  • Monitor usage for safety and reliability
4

AI Processing

ALICE AI uses artificial intelligence to generate responses. Your inputs may be processed by secure cloud-based infrastructure and trusted AI service providers. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data.

5

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under:

  • Consent – when you create an account and use the app
  • Legitimate interests – to improve and maintain the service
6

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share data with cloud hosting providers, AI processing providers, analytics and performance tools, and legal authorities where required. All providers are required to meet data protection standards.

7

Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the app’s functionality, improve the service, and meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

8

Your Rights

You have the right to access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion, and restrict processing. Contact: hello@alice-ai.co.uk

9

Children’s Data

ALICE AI is designed for use by parents and carers. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children. All child-related data is entered by adults.

10

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including secure storage, controlled access, and encryption where appropriate.

11

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for hosting, AI processing, and analytics. These providers process data on our behalf and under strict data protection obligations.

12

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users will be notified of significant changes where appropriate.

13

Contact
